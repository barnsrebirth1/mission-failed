how to play

The mission: drive to the yellow safehouse ⌂. Follow the yellow arrow on screen and the yellow dot on your map.
Don't stop near cops. If a cop is on you and you're stopped, the blue/red bar fills — full bar = busted.
Don't get rammed to bits. The green bar is your health — empty = wasted.
